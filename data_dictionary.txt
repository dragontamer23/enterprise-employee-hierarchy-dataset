ENTERPRISE EMPLOYEE & CORPORATE HIERARCHY DATASET
Version: 1.0 (Cleaned & Normalized)
Records: 5,200+
File format: CSV (UTF-8)

SCHEMA DESCRIPTION:
- Employee_ID: Unique identifier string for each employee profile.
- Name: Full employee name (standard title case, trimmed).
- Department: Operating functional division (e.g., Engineering, Sales, Product).
- Company_ID: Internal corporate identifier.
- Job_Title: Official role and corporate title.
- Seniority_Level: Organizational level (Executive, Director, Manager, Senior, Entry).
- Education_Level: Verified academic tier (Bachelors, Masters, PhD).
- Work_Location: Geographic office assignment.
- Influence_Score: Internal ranking metric for org hierarchy mapping.
- Decision_Maker_Flag: Boolean indicator (1 = Budget/Decision authority, 0 = Non-authority).
- Preferred_Contact_Method: Designated outreach preference.
- Last_Contact_Date / Next_Followup_Date: Structured pipeline touchpoints.
- Active_Flag: Employment status indicator.
